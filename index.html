PHOLENISMO Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

PHOLENISMO respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the PHOLENISMO mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Name – such as the name provided for a reservation.
  • Phone Number – used for reservation and trip-related communication.
  • Payment Information – such as the selected payment method and payment reference provided for a reservation.
  • User Content – such as reviews or other content submitted through the app.

How We Use Information

  • Process and manage trip reservations.
  • Display and manage booking information.
  • Support trip and event organization.
  • Manage van rental requests.
  • Provide app functionality and respond to reservation-related needs.

Data Storage

PHOLENISMO uses third-party cloud services, including Supabase, to store and manage application data.

Tracking and Advertising

PHOLENISMO does not use personal information for tracking purposes and does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that support the operation of PHOLENISMO, such as its cloud database infrastructure.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's services, manage reservations, maintain records, or comply with applicable requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding PHOLENISMO, please use the support contact information provided in the App Store listing.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
